# watchedThis

*[watchedthis.com](https://watchedthis.com)**

Tired of spending 20 minutes deciding what to watch? Yeah, same. watchedThis fixes that.

It's a movie and TV show discovery platform that actually makes the process enjoyable — whether you know exactly what you want, have no idea, or are trying to figure out the name of that one movie from a screenshot you saved 3 years ago.

---

## What it does

**Movie Spinner**
Can't decide? Spin it. Get a random pick based on your taste — not just a random movie from a list.

**PlotAlike Finder**
Loved a movie but don't know what to watch next? Describe the plot or pick a title and get recommendations that actually match the vibe.

**Poster Scanner**
See a poster somewhere and curious what it is? Snap it or upload it and get the full details instantly.

**Scene Detector**
Got a screenshot or image from a movie or show but have no idea what it's from? Upload it and watchedThis will identify the title for you — no guessing, no reverse-searching on Google.

**Watchlist**
Save anything you find interesting for later. Sign in with Google and your list follows you everywhere.

**Filters**
Genre, mood, rating, language — narrow it down however you want.

Everything — trailers, cast, reviews, ratings — is pulled in real time. No stale data.
